About Us

Who We Are

We, Bondhu , began our journey on 15th August 2017, started by a few college-going students with a simple belief — small acts of kindness can create lasting impact. What started as a small initiative has grown into a dedicated effort to serve communities across Bengal with compassion and care.

Over the years, our work has taken shape through initiatives like Bhalobeshe Ahare, Nivedan, Upohar, Himel Upohar, Oder Pujo, Roktodan Utsab, Rangamatir Hansi, and Disaster Relief. As we continue to grow, we look forward to expanding our impact with our upcoming initiative, Raktosetu.

At BONDHU, we remain committed to building a world where kindness is shared and no one is left behind.

Event History

Our Journey in Events

A timeline of milestones, drives, and moments that have defined Bondhu's mission across the years.

🎉
2017 — Founding

Bondhu is Born

A group of passionate young individuals in Ramrajatala came together with one dream — to be a true friend to the community. Bondhu was officially founded.

Milestone
🍚
2018 — First Drive

First Food Distribution Drive

Bondhu conducted its first formal food distribution, delivering meals and rations to over 80 daily-wage families in the Ramrajatala locality.

Food Relief
👕
November 2019

Winter Clothing Drive

Collected and distributed blankets, woolens, and warm clothing to over 150 people across Howrah ahead of the winter season.

Clothing
🦠
2020 — COVID Response

Pandemic Relief Operations

During the COVID-19 lockdowns, Bondhu distributed dry rations, masks, and sanitizers to hundreds of vulnerable households in Howrah's most affected localities.

Disaster Relief
🌀
May 2021 — Cyclone Yaas

Cyclone Yaas Emergency Relief

Mobilized within hours of Cyclone Yaas striking West Bengal — distributed food packets, tarpaulins, and emergency kits to displaced families.

Disaster Relief
📚
September 2022

Books & School Supplies Drive

Supplied school books, bags, and stationery to 80+ underprivileged students ahead of the new academic year, giving their education a fresh start.

Education
👕
November 2022

Winter Clothing Drive

Distributed blankets, woolens, and warm clothing to over 300 people — our largest clothing drive to date.

Clothing
🌊
July 2023

Flood Relief — Howrah Response

Emergency response during the 2023 floods — dry food packets, drinking water, and medicines distributed to flood-hit families across Howrah.

Disaster Relief
🩺
December 2023

Free Health Check-up Camp

A free medical camp with local doctors, providing consultations, medicines, and health tests to 150+ residents in Ramrajatala.

Healthcare
🍱
January 2024

Winter Food Distribution Drive

Distributed hot meals and food packets to over 200 families and daily-wage laborers in Ramrajatala and nearby areas during peak winter.

Food Relief

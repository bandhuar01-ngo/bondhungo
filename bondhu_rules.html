Rules & Regulations

  • 1

    Every member must adhere to all the rules and regulations stated in the Articles of Association for the Trust of the organization.

  • 2

    During any project, members must strictly follow the decisions made by the Project Head, Sub-Committee, or Core Committee.

  • 3

    Members will not have any voting rights in the Annual General Meeting (AGM); however, their opinions for the betterment of the organization will be warmly welcomed.

  • 4

    Each member must pay a monthly subscription of ₹50 (₹600 annually).

  • 5

    Members are required to be present in at least half of the total projects conducted during the financial year.

  • 6

    Certificates will be awarded at the Annual General Meeting (AGM) based on members' performance.

  • 7

    Each member will be provided with an identity card (ID Card).

  • 8

    Volunteership must be renewed every 2 years, and the renewal process will be completely free of cost.

  • 9

    Members will be informed in advance about all projects of the organization.

  • 10

    Active cooperation from all members is highly expected.

  • 11

    If any member violates the rules of the organization, the Core Committee will take immediate action, and their decision will be considered final.
